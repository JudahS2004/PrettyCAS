What can I type?

Type real math notation with a keyboard (x^2, sqrt(16), x_1); the field turns it into proper notation as you type. Or use the keyboard icon for a symbol palette.

Numbers has a third option, Engineering: same decimal evaluation, but the exponent is always a multiple of 3 (12.3e6, not 1.23e7).

The Compute page keeps a Workspace below the output. Type r = 5 and r is remembered for later inputs (r + 2 evaluates to 7) until you clear it or reload.
